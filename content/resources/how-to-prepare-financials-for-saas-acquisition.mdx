- Monthly P&L and balance sheet
- ARR/MRR bridge
- Cohort retention and churn definitions
- Revenue concentration analysis

## ARR bridge and revenue quality

Build a monthly ARR bridge showing new, expansion, contraction, and churn. Separate usage spikes from recurring growth.

## Examples: strong vs weak financial prep

### Example 1: Strong prep

- ARR bridge reconciles to P&L
- Cohorts show stable retention
- Diligence completed in 60 days

### Example 2: Weak prep

- Inconsistent ARR definitions
- Missing churn breakdown
- Price retrade due to uncertainty

## Common mistakes

1. **Mixing bookings and ARR.**
2. **Not documenting churn definitions.**
3. **Ignoring revenue concentration risk.**

## Action checklist

- [ ] Build a 24-month ARR bridge.
- [ ] Reconcile ARR to revenue recognition.
- [ ] Document churn and NRR definitions.
- [ ] Prepare a summary KPI pack.

## FAQs

**What financials do buyers need for SaaS?**
Monthly P&Ls, ARR bridges, churn cohorts, and revenue concentration analyses are standard.

**How do I build an ARR bridge?**
Track starting ARR plus new, expansion, contraction, and churn each month, then reconcile to reported revenue.

**How long does financial prep take?**
Plan for 4–8 weeks depending on data cleanliness.
