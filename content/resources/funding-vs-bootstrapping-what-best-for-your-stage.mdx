## Pros and cons

- **Funding:** faster scale, dilution, higher growth expectations.
- **Bootstrapping:** control, slower scale, more capital constraints.

## Examples: two paths

### Example 1: Bootstrapped SaaS

- $50k MRR, 20% monthly growth
- Prioritized profitability and retained ownership

### Example 2: Venture-backed SaaS

- $1M ARR, 80% YoY growth
- Raised to capture fast-moving enterprise market

## Common mistakes

1. **Raising before PMF.**
2. **Bootstrapping in a winner-take-all market.**
3. **Ignoring runway when deciding.**

## FAQs

**Should I bootstrap or raise?**
Bootstrap if you can reach profitability and the market is not winner-take-all. Raise if speed is a competitive advantage and you can deploy capital efficiently.

**When should SaaS raise funding?**
After you have clear PMF and predictable growth levers.

**How much runway should I have?**
Aim for 12–18 months to give flexibility for fundraising or growth pivots.
