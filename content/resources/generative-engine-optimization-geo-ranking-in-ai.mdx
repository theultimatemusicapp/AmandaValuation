## FAQs

**What is GEO?**
Generative Engine Optimization focuses on ranking inside AI-generated answers rather than only in traditional search results.

**How is GEO different from SEO?**
SEO focuses on link-based ranking, while GEO emphasizes trust, citations, and clear answer structures.

**What signals matter most?**
Credible sources, structured answers, and consistent topical authority.
