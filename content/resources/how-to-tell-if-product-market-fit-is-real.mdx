# How to Tell If Product-Market Fit Is Real

PMF is not a vibe—it is a set of signals that customers will pay, stay, and expand. This guide shows the metrics and evidence that prove the fit is real.

## Core PMF signals

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[High activation] --> B[Retention cohorts stabilize]
    B --> C[Expansion revenue]
    C --> D[Organic referrals]
```

- Customers reach activation quickly.
- Retention cohorts flatten instead of collapsing.
- Expansion revenue grows without heavy discounting.

## Metrics that validate fit

- **Retention:** 6–12 month cohorts flatten.
- **NRR:** 100%+ signals expansion.
- **Payback:** CAC payback < 12–18 months.

## Examples: real vs false PMF

### Example 1: Real PMF

- Activation: 70% within 7 days
- NRR: 112%
- Pipeline driven by referrals

### Example 2: False PMF

- High signups but 3% monthly churn
- Discounts required to close deals
- Low usage after month two

## Common mistakes

1. **Confusing signups with PMF.**
2. **Ignoring retention in older cohorts.**
3. **Chasing multiple segments too early.**

## Action checklist

- [ ] Define your ideal customer profile (ICP).
- [ ] Track cohort retention monthly.
- [ ] Measure activation time-to-value.
- [ ] Interview churned customers.
- [ ] Validate expansion in mature cohorts.

## FAQs

**How do I know if I have product-market fit?**
PMF is real when retention stabilizes, customers expand, and referrals grow without heavy incentives.

**What metrics show PMF?**
Cohort retention, NRR above 100%, and fast activation are the most reliable indicators.

**What are false PMF signals?**
High signups with poor retention, heavy discounting, and inconsistent usage often indicate false PMF.
