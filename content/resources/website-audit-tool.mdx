# Website Audit Tool: Find Conversion, Trust, and SEO Gaps

Your website is often the first diligence screen investors and buyers run. The Website Audit tool turns that surface review into a structured checklist, scorecard, and prioritized fixes.

## What the Website Audit covers

1. **Positioning & clarity:** Does the hero section clearly state who you help and the outcome?
2. **Conversion & UX:** Are CTAs obvious, pricing easy to find, and flows friction-free?
3. **Trust & credibility:** Are there testimonials, security proof, and legal links?
4. **Technical SEO basics:** Titles, meta descriptions, canonical tags, headings, sitemap/robots.
5. **Investor readiness:** Risk flags like short runway, weak margin, or high churn.

## How the audit works

- Crawl the submitted URL + up to five internal pages (home, pricing, product, about, blog).
- Extract technical signals and trust/conversion heuristics.
- Return a ranked list of issues with fixes plus quick wins by timeline.

## What to do after the audit

- **Fix conversion leaks:** move the primary CTA above the fold and add ROI proof.
- **Add trust signals:** customer logos, testimonials, and security badges near key CTAs.
- **Improve SEO basics:** fill in titles, descriptions, canonical tags, and sitemap.
