## Risk scoring framework

Score each area from 1 (low risk) to 5 (high risk) and prioritize the top two fixes first.

## Examples: before and after fixes

### Example 1: Concentration risk

- Before: top customer 22% of ARR
- After: added 4 new mid-market accounts, top customer down to 12%

### Example 2: Founder dependency

- Before: founder approves every enterprise deal
- After: sales leader and SOPs reduced dependency score

## Common mistakes

1. **Ignoring concentration until diligence.**
2. **Assuming security risk won’t surface.**
3. **Not documenting processes.**

## Action checklist

- [ ] Calculate top-customer concentration.
- [ ] Trend churn and NRR by cohort.
- [ ] Document key operational processes.

## FAQs

**What are SaaS red flags for buyers?**
High concentration, churn volatility, founder dependency, and security gaps are the most common.

**How do I assess risk in my SaaS?**
Score revenue, retention, operations, and security, then prioritize fixes for the highest scores.

**What risks reduce valuation?**
Anything that makes revenue less durable or the business harder to transfer to a new owner.
