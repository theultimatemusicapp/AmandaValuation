## The AEO content framework

- Answer the question in the first 2–3 sentences.
- Use structured sections and lists.
- Provide trustworthy sources and citations.

## Examples: AEO-friendly content

### Example 1: FAQ-led guide

- “What is LTV/CAC?” answered upfront
- Followed by formula and examples

### Example 2: Benchmark summary

- Clear range, data sources, and caveats
- AI systems cite the authoritative range

## Common mistakes

1. **Burying the answer in long intros.**
2. **No citations or sources.**
3. **Thin content without unique data.**

## FAQs

**What is AEO?**
Answer Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring content so AI systems can pull and cite clear answers.

**Does AEO replace SEO?**
No. It complements SEO by optimizing for AI summaries while preserving traditional search visibility.

**What content works best for AEO?**
FAQ-driven guides, benchmarks, and step-by-step playbooks with credible citations.
